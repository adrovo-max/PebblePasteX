# What's new

## 1.0.7 — October 8, 2026

- Your keyboard/mouse and text, file, and folder sharing switches now keep their last state when the app restarts. Turning them off is remembered too.
- Pause and tray Exit no longer mean setting up your switches again next time.
- A confirmed reset in Settings clears only the two sharing switches, leaving pairing and other preferences intact.
- The Visit HandyPebble link is now at the top of the app, where it is easier to find.
- New settings messages are available in all 12 supported languages.

First-time setup still requires you to enable sharing. After upgrading from an older version, choose your switches once so the app can remember them.
